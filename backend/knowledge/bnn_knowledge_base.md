## 1. Core BNN Principles & Methodology
**Source: "Day 1 and 2 notes BNN.pdf", "BNN Bootcamp 1.pdf"**

### 1.1 What Makes BNN Different from Traditional Vedic Astrology
- **No Lagna (Ascendant) and No Dasha system.** Traditional Vedic astrology depends heavily on exact birth time (Lagna changes every ~2 hours) and Moon's precise degree (for Dasha calculations). BNN — as taught by sage Nandishwar — rejects both because most people historically did not know their exact birth time, making Lagna/Dasha unreliable.
- **Jupiter (Guru) is used as the anchor of the chart instead of the Ascendant.** Jupiter is the **Jeeva Karaka** — the significator of the self / life force (Prana Shakti). Jupiter *represents the native* — "it is you."
- **90% of BNN predictions are derived from planets and their combinations** (not house/sign dispositor logic as in mainstream Vedic astrology).
- BNN is relatively **insensitive to small birth-time errors** (except for Moon's degree), because most planets move slowly; the Directional Chart structure holds even if birth time is off by a few hours.

### 1.2 Rule #1 — Jupiter as the First House ("Jeeva Lagna")
- Wherever Jupiter is placed in the birth chart, that **sign (Rashi)** becomes the **First House** ("Jeeva Lagna").
- Houses are counted **anti-clockwise** from Jupiter's sign: the next sign = 2nd house, next = 3rd house, etc., through all 12 houses.
- Important distinction: **houses and signs are different concepts.** If Jupiter is in Capricorn, we say "the First House is in Capricorn" — not "the First House IS Capricorn." The sign identity (Capricorn) is retained for sign-based readings (health body-part mapping, etc.) while the house count is separately derived from Jupiter.
- Jupiter's retrograde status has **no effect** on the house-counting direction.

### 1.3 Rule #2 — Planets in Trine Are Treated as Conjunct
- This is **the most important rule** in BNN and underlies most predictions.
- A **trine** = signs 120° apart (1-5-9 relationship). Planets sitting in trine signs to each other are treated **as if conjunct** (forming a "combination").
- **The Four Trines (Elemental Groups) — the "Directional Chart":**
  | Trine | Houses | Element | Direction |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 1, 5, 9 | Fire (Agni) | Aries, Leo, Sagittarius | East (Top) |
  | 2, 6, 10 | Earth (Prithvi) | Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn | South (Right) |
  | 3, 7, 11 | Air (Vayu) | Gemini, Libra, Aquarius | West (Bottom) |
  | 4, 8, 12 | Water (Jal) | Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces | North (Left) |
  - Energy flow moves clockwise: East → South → West → North.
- Within a trine, planets are arranged in **ascending order of degree** (lowest degree first). Degree does **not** indicate strength — only sequencing (who is "ahead"/"behind").
- If two planets share the exact same degree, the one with the **lower minutes** comes first.

### 1.4 Rule #3 — Plan & Action (Forward Movement / "Ahead")
- A planet ahead (higher degree in same trine, OR simply being in the next house/2nd house — the 2nd house planet is *always* considered "ahead" regardless of degree, since a sign only spans 0–30°) reveals the **plan/action/future** relative to the planet behind it.
- The planet with lower degree "moves toward" (takes on qualities of) the planet with higher degree.
- Example: Sun (Father) at 2°, Venus (wealth) at 10°, same trine → after native's birth, father gains wealth/luxury/vehicles.

### 1.5 Rule #4 — Past & History (Backward Movement / "Behind")
- The corollary of Rule #3: a planet behind another (lower degree in same trine, OR in the previous/12th house) signifies the **past/history** relative to that native's birth.
- Example: Jupiter at 5°, Moon at 2° (behind) → *before* native's birth, the family experienced a significant change (house/city move) signified by Moon.

### 1.6 Rule #5 — 7th House Aspect (Support & Modification)
- A planet in the 7th house from another planet **aspects** it — mutual, simultaneous influence (unlike trine, cannot be sequenced by degree).
- **Friendly vs. inimical groups:**
  - **Dev Grah (Divine group):** Sun, Moon, Mars, Jupiter, Ketu — friends among themselves.
  - **Danav Grah (Demonic group):** Saturn, Venus, Mercury, Rahu — friends among themselves.
  - **Exceptions:** Sun–Mercury are friends; Jupiter–Saturn are friendly; Jupiter–Mercury are friendly; Moon–Mars (both Dev Grah) can still have negative combined effects.
- **Effect:** Friendly aspect → planets enhance each other's positive qualities (e.g., Sun opposite Jupiter → name, fame, wisdom). Inimical aspect → planets obstruct/negatively modify each other (e.g., Saturn opposite Mars → professional frustration unless native works in a Mars-related field).

### 1.7 Rule #6 — Rahu & Ketu Special Aspect Rules
- Rahu and Ketu are always 180° apart and always retrograde.
- They have **trinal aspects** (5th & 9th) like all planets.
- Because always retrograde, they have a **powerful 12th-house aspect** — a planet in the 12th from Rahu/Ketu is treated as tightly conjunct with them.
- **No mutual 7th-house aspect between Rahu and Ketu** — a planet conjunct Rahu has no automatic connection to Ketu and vice versa (prevents contradictory predictions since Rahu = expansion, Ketu = contraction, opposites).

### 1.8 Rule #7 — Transfer of Qualities (Karakatva)
- Restatement/emphasis of Rule #3: the planet ahead (by degree or next house) transfers its qualities to the planet behind it, **regardless of friend/enemy status**.
- Example: Sun (Father) followed by Moon (change/food) → father's life undergoes major change (job/location) after native's birth, or father is in food/hospitality business.

### 1.9 Rule #8 — Determining a Planet's Strength
- A planet's strength/support is determined by presence of planets in its **2nd, 12th, and 7th houses**.
- **Strong planet:** has friendly planets in these positions — well-supported, can overcome challenges.
- **Weak/isolated planet:** no planets in 2nd/12th/7th — lacks support, creates significant life challenges in that planet's domain.
  - Example: isolated Saturn → career struggles, lack of direction, unwillingness to work. Isolated Jupiter → phobias, fear, feeling of no support.
- Even **inimical planets present are better than none** — they provide challenges to overcome, which beats having no direction.

### 1.10 Rule #9 — Retrograde Planets
- A retrograde planet gives the results of the **previous house** (moves backward).
- Movement: it first interacts (within its own trine) with planets at a **lower degree**. Then it "moves" into the **previous trine**, entering from the 30° mark and interacting with planets there **in descending order of degree** (highest degree first).
- Worked example: Jupiter (21° R) and Mars (6°) in 4-8-12 trine; previous trine (3-7-11) has Saturn (28°) and Ketu (8°).
  1. Jupiter (R) → meets Mars (6°, lower degree, within trine): combination Jupiter+Mars.
  2. Moves to previous trine (3-7-11), entering from 30°, meets highest degree first: Saturn (28°): combination Jupiter+Saturn.
  3. Continues backward, meets Ketu (8°): combination Jupiter+Ketu.
  - Interpretation: this single Jupiter gives combined results of Mars, Saturn, and Ketu.

### 1.11 Additional Operating Rules
- **Multiple planets in the next house:** the primary planet forms combinations with all of them, starting from the lowest degree.
- **Sequence of analysis:** (1) analyze the trine first; (2) if nothing ahead by degree, check the 2nd house; (3) check 7th house aspects; (4) check 12th house for past influences; (5) apply retrograde rules where applicable.
- **Isolated planets** (alone in trine, nothing in 2nd/12th/7th) = very weak/unsupported → fear and lack of progress in that domain.
- **Special client technique:** Ask a client to pick a number 1–70 (referring to the 70 planetary pair combinations catalogued in Sections 2.1–2.3) — that combination answers their query (a divination/cartomancy-style shortcut).

## 4. Planetary Combinations (Yogas) — The 70 Combinations
**Source: "BNN Bootcamp 3 Combinations Part1.pdf" (1–24), "BNN Bootcamp 4 notes.pdf" (25–30, duplicate subset of Bootcamp 5), "BNN Bootcamp 5 Combinations Part2.pdf" (25–44), "BNN bootcamp 6 Combination of planets Part3.pdf" (45–70)**

**Methodology recap:** For any two planets in trine (or conjunct/2nd-house pair), arrange by degree — lower degree (5° in examples) = "first"/behind, higher degree (10° in examples) = "second"/ahead. The planet ahead gives its *Jeeva Karaka* reading; when interpreting, one planet's living (Jeeva) significations combine with the other's non-living (Ajeeva) significations, and vice versa depending on direction. Each combination is studied in **both orders** (X+Y and Y+X) because which planet is "ahead" changes whether the described events happen *before* or *after* the native's birth (or before/after marriage, etc.).

## 6. Transits
**Source: "BNN Bootcamp 8 Transits.pdf"**

### 6.1 Core Concept
- The natal chart (Kundli) is a fixed snapshot at birth; planets continuously move through the zodiac afterward — this ongoing movement is **transit**.
- BNN uses transit primarily to identify a **nearby time window** for events, not exact dates — "we don't predict exact date and month, nor do we intend to."
- Transits of **Jupiter and Saturn** are the primary tools used.

### 6.2 Approximate Transit Durations per Sign

|---|---|

| Moon | ~2.5 days |
| Mars | ~45 days |
| Mercury | ~1 month |
| Jupiter | ~1 year |
| Venus | ~1 month |
| Saturn | ~2.5 years |
| Rahu | ~1.5 years |
| Ketu | ~1.5 years |
(Retrograde periods can add days/months to these.)

### 6.3 Natal vs. Transit
- **Natal planets:** fixed positions from the birth chart.
- **Transiting planets:** current real-time positions in the sky (checked via a "today's date" chart or a transit app such as "Graha Transits").

### 6.4 Jupiter Transit vs. Saturn Transit — Nature
- **Jupiter transit = free will.** E.g., transiting Jupiter over natal Venus → person may *work hard* to earn money, take a bank job, marry (male chart), or pursue entertainment.
- **Saturn transit = fate/destiny/action.** E.g., transiting Saturn over natal Venus → *bound* to give money/house/luxury/job/prosperity — but cannot deliver if free will (during the Jupiter phase) was wasted. "As you sow, so you reap."

### 6.5 Transit Impact Weighting Rules (both Jupiter and Saturn)
1. Transiting planet gives result of the natal planet in the **same rashi** — **100% impact**.
2. Result of the **7th aspect/dristi** — **80% impact** (for Jupiter); for Saturn, the aspect houses are **3rd, 7th, and 10th** — **80% impact**.
3. Result of **trinal rashis** (1-5-9 pattern) — **50% impact**.
4. **3rd and 11th houses** from the transiting planet's position are also activated — **25% impact**.

## 7. Progression
**Source: "Bnn Bootcamp 9 Progression.pdf"**

### 7.1 The Jupiter Progression System (12-Year Rounds)
BNN's timing technique advances through the houses from Jupiter in **12-year rounds**, each round corresponding sequentially to houses 1 through 6 (i.e., through Jupiter's full 12-year zodiac cycle, ~6 cycles = 72 years, after which "free will" ends and Saturn/Fate/Karma dominates).

| Round | Age Range | House Focus | Life Themes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | 0–12 | 1st house | Self, Health, School |
| 2nd | 12–24 | 2nd house | Family, Speech, Education, Finances |
| 3rd | 24–36 | 3rd house | Courage, Risk, Marriage, direction of life, Loss |
| 4th | 36–48 | 4th house | Home, Happiness, Property, Mother |
| 5th | 48–60 | 5th house | Children, settlement, Purva Punya, Habits |
| 6th | 60–72 | 6th house | Purva Paap, liability, disease, debt, enemies |
| — | 72+ | — | Jupiter's (free will) role ends; Fate/Karma (Saturn) takes over |

### 7.2 Rules for Building the "String" of Planets for a Round
For each 12-year round, build a sequential "string" of relevant planets by checking, in order:
1. Planet(s) physically in the concerned house (e.g., Round 1 → 1st house from Jupiter).
2. Planet(s) in conjunction in that house.
3. Planet(s) in trine to that house.
4. Planet(s) giving 7th aspect to that house.
5. The dispositor (Rashi lord) of the sign occupying that house.
6. Planet(s) conjunct with that dispositor.
7. Planet(s) giving 7th aspect to the dispositor.
8. Any exchanges among the planets identified so far.
- The resulting string is neither wholly good nor bad: **more Destiny Breakers (Sa/Ra/Ke) in the string = harsher period; more Destiny Makers (Su/Ve/Me/Ju) = more favorable period.**
- Within each 12-year round, the individual years are further sub-divided (roughly year-by-year) and specific life events are matched to specific planets in the string — demonstrated via detailed worked case-study examples in the source (a female native's chart tracked event-by-event through all 6 rounds, e.g., "2–3 years: change of city," "25–26 years: marriage, foreign travel," "67–68 years: cancer relapse and death.") These worked examples function as calibration/training cases rather than universal rules.

### 7.3 Varshphal (Annual Chart) Technique
1. Determine the client's **current running age** (e.g., turned 24, so 25th year is running).
2. **Count from Jupiter's position** up to that running-age number (Jupiter's own sign = count 1, then continue counting signs sequentially — anti-clockwise, per Rule #1 house-counting convention).
3. The sign landed on becomes the "rashi for this year" (Varshphal sign) — used for annual predictions.

BNN analyzes any specific life domain (profession, business, education) by building a sequential "string" of planetary influences for that domain's significator planet — described as a "karmic thread" connecting the planet to its environment, actions, and outcomes.

### 10.2 Saturn's String — Profession
- **Placement:** the Rashi Saturn occupies defines the primary flavor of the native's work life.
- **Conjunctions & Trines:** planets in the same Rashi as Saturn, and those in trine (1st/5th/9th from Saturn), shape the profession further.
- **Retrograde note:** if Saturn is retrograde, the previous trine must also be considered for complete professional analysis.
- **Work Environment & Action:**
  - **12th house from Saturn** → signifies the work environment / latent conditions.
  - **2nd house from Saturn** → shows the plan/action of the profession — "the next step of the soul."
- **Professional Timeline via Saturn's aspects:**
  - **3rd aspect** = the Start (initial efforts, career beginnings).
  - **7th aspect** = the Middle (peak performance, expansion).
  - **10th aspect** = the End (ultimate result, culmination of work).

### 10.3 Mercury's String — Education & Business
- **Placement:** Rashi Mercury occupies.
- **Connections:** planets conjunct Mercury or in trine to it.
- **Retrograde note:** if retrograde, always check the previous trine for "hidden intelligence."
- **Directional aspects:** 7th, 11th, and 3rd houses from Mercury show where business expands.
- **12th house from Mercury** → signifies educational environment / school-college background.
- **2nd house from Mercury** → indicates surroundings and immediate support for business ventures.

### 10.4 Saturn + Mercury Integration
Both strings should be checked together: "Education and business are inseparable parts of the karmic cycle. One provides the tools (Mercury), and the other provides the field for action (Saturn)." Tagline from the source: *"Saturn is the Action, Mercury is the Strategy. Together they define the Destiny."*

### 10.7 The 12th House Rule — Work/Study Environment
Always check the 12th house relative to the significator (Saturn for profession, Mercury for education/business):
- **Poor environment:** Mars, Rahu, or Ketu in the 12th from Saturn/Mercury → difficult/harsh work or study environment.
- **Superior environment:** Venus, Jupiter, or Sun in the 12th from Saturn/Mercury → native enjoys work/studies, thrives in the setting.

### 10.9 Career Progress Timing (via Transit)
- **First income:** In Saturn's 1st round (age 0–30), when transiting Saturn crosses natal Venus (1st income/job indicator), or the 7th from Venus, or trine to Venus.
- **Progress in profession:** Saturn transits over (same rashi), 7th, or trine of Venus, Sun+Mars, Sun, Mercury, or Jupiter.
- **Troubles in profession:** Saturn transits over (same rashi), 7th, or trine of Rahu (initial issues), Ketu (job loss/change/troubles), Mars (conflicts/loss), Saturn (extra hard work), or Moon (change/transfer/travel).

### 10.10 Education-Specific Notes
- **Troubles in education:** if malefic planets (Rahu, Ketu, Saturn, Mars) lie between the path from Jupiter to Mercury, hurdles in education occur; malefics between Venus and Mercury hinder smoothness of education.
- **Foreign education indicators:**
  - Mercury's connection with Rahu.
  - Ketu can also send the native abroad but tends to bring them back after studies (no permanent settlement).
  - Retrograde Jupiter or Mercury.
